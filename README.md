# 🚁 ResQ-Swarm

> **Explainable AI-Based Rescue Prioritization Using Drone Imagery**

An Explainable AI decision-support platform that helps emergency responders analyze RGB and thermal drone imagery to detect victims, assess visible urgency, and generate a transparent, human-verifiable rescue-priority queue.

> 🏆 **Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026** • Drones & Robotics

---

## 📖 Overview

During disasters, drones can rapidly capture aerial imagery, but identifying **who needs immediate rescue** remains a major challenge.

**ResQ-Swarm** transforms RGB and thermal drone imagery into actionable rescue intelligence by combining victim detection, posture analysis, hazard assessment, confidence scoring, and explainable AI. Instead of replacing responders, the system assists them with transparent recommendations while keeping humans in complete control of rescue decisions.

---

## ✨ Features

- 👤 AI-powered victim detection
- 🌡 RGB & Thermal data fusion
- 🧍 Posture & movement analysis
- 🔥 Hazard-aware prioritization
- 📊 Confidence scoring
- 🧠 Explainable AI recommendations
- 🗺 Interactive rescue dashboard
- ✅ Human verification & override
